# KROVINOX image selection and review standard

The catalog is an apparel research portfolio. Images must support the specific garment entry and must not make a reference item look like stock that KROVINOX owns or sells.

## Before an image is accepted

- **Source match:** the product page and image must show the same garment and colour. A category page is labelled as a category-level reference; it must not be described as a verified individual product.
- **Professional image quality:** use the retailer's largest practical image variant (target at least 900 pixels on the long edge where provided). Reject visibly compressed, soft, pixelated, over-sharpened, watermarked, or distorted images.
- **People and portrait framing:** if a model is visible, the face should be naturally rendered and in focus when it is meant to be seen. Avoid crops that cut awkwardly through the face, eyes, or chin; avoid frame grabs with blink/blur, distracting expressions, or visibly poor lighting. Judge the photograph's craft and relevance, not the person's identity or personal traits.
- **Premium art direction:** favour deliberate posture, coherent styling, controlled lighting, clean backgrounds, and a clear view of the garment's shape and construction. Avoid images that read like casual marketplace snapshots when a retailer campaign image exists.
- **Garment legibility:** seams, collar, pocket, hem and fit should be visible when they are discussed. A crop must not imply details that cannot be seen.
- **Truthful metadata:** never infer fibre percentages, fit measurements, or performance from appearance alone. Link the original product source and distinguish retailer claims from visual observations.
- **Consistent presentation:** use a restrained editorial crop and consistent colour treatment. Do not apply filters that alter the garment's true colour, and do not retouch or reconstruct a person's face.

## Scroll-study rule

The existing scroll study is a 2D editorial separation built from one source photograph, not a true 3D garment model. Labels must describe only visible, verifiable features. The sequence can be promoted to a physically accurate exploded view only after suitable multi-view imagery or a licensed/modelled 3D asset is available.

## Review gate before launch

Review the homepage at desktop and narrow mobile widths, then inspect the complete catalog. Check image load success, source correspondence, face/portrait framing, fabric detail, crop consistency, and colour fidelity. Replace any weak source image rather than hiding its problems with filters. The current references remain portfolio-only and should stay noindex until launch facts and image-use rights are verified.
