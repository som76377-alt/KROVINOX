# Typography and hierarchy audit

1. Capture the real page at 1440x900, 390x844, and 320px wide.
2. Name the first, second, and third visual anchors. Check that they match the page purpose and next action.
3. Check each text role: display heading, section heading, body, product title, product status, navigation, caption, and metadata.
4. Check size and weight contrast, line-height, paragraph measure, tracking, and intentional wrapping.
5. Audit vertical rhythm from eyebrow to heading, body, CTA, image caption, section title, grid, and footer.
6. Check alignment across header, hero, content, product grid, and footer. Asymmetry must be composed, not accidental.
7. Check contrast and readability over images, image crops, aspect ratios, and alt text.
8. Check the primary CTA: specific label, visible focus, adequate touch target, and meaningful destination.
9. Check keyboard access and reduced-motion behavior.
10. Record problems by severity, fix the highest-impact issues, capture screenshots again, and inspect the new render.

Reject generic hero claims, random micro-labels, fake social proof, decorative gradients, pill buttons, repetitive card shells, excessive scroll animation, and conclusions based only on source code.
